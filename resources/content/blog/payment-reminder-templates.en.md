Writing a payment reminder seems easy until you sit down to write it. Too soft? Too awkward? Here are **five ready templates**, one for each stage. Replace the brackets with your details and send.

## 1. Three days before the due date (friendly)

**WhatsApp:**

> Hi [Client name], a quick note from [Your company]. Invoice [Invoice number] for AED [Amount] is due on [Date]. To pay by transfer: [IBAN]. Thank you for working with us.

## 2. On the due date

**Email:**

> **Subject:** Invoice [Invoice number] is due today
>
> Hi [Client name],
>
> A reminder that invoice [Invoice number] for AED [Amount] is due today. A copy is attached. If you have already paid, please ignore this message, and thank you.
>
> Best regards, [Your name]

## 3. One week after the due date (gentle follow-up)

**WhatsApp:**

> Hi [Client name], following up on invoice [Invoice number] for AED [Amount], which was due on [Date]. Is there anything we can help with to complete the payment?

"Is there anything we can help with" invites the client to tell you the real reason for the delay: a missing copy, a missing PO number, or a pending approval.

## 4. After 30 days (clearer)

**Email:**

> **Subject:** Follow-up: invoice [Invoice number] is 30 days overdue
>
> Hi [Client name],
>
> We have not yet received payment for invoice [Invoice number] for AED [Amount], due since [Date]. Please arrange payment within the next seven days, or let us know when we can expect it.
>
> Best regards, [Your name]

## 5. Final notice (after 45 days)

**Email:**

> **Subject:** Final notice: invoice [Invoice number]
>
> Hi [Client name],
>
> This is a final notice regarding invoice [Invoice number] for AED [Amount], overdue since [Date]. Please pay or contact us before [Specific date] to avoid any further action.
>
> Best regards, [Your name]

## Tips for using these templates

- **Always personalise the name and amount.** Generic messages are easy to ignore.
- **Send on working-day mornings**, when accounts teams review payments.
- **Never send the same message twice.** Each follow-up should be slightly clearer than the last.
- **Keep a record of every message**: when, to whom, and on which channel.
