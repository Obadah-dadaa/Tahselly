# Tahselly teaser: storylines

The one thing: any UAE business that invoices on credit (30/60/90 days) stops chasing payments by hand. Tahselly reminds the client and the money comes in.
Viewer's words afterwards: "This would do the chasing for my company too."
Company names are illustrative. Amounts and screens come from the real app and the seeded demo company.

## A. "نفس القصة بكل الإمارات": Life before/after × split screen to one (RECOMMENDED)
Three UAE businesses everyone knows, side by side on the same morning: a general trading company in Deira, a maintenance (AMC) company in Al Quoz, and a marketing agency in Business Bay. Each owner looks at the same problem: unpaid invoices, a client who doesn't answer, a phone full of "please pay" drafts. The three panes collapse into one Tahselly screen. Each business then gets the feature that fits it: the trading company imports 200 invoices from Excel, the maintenance company has WhatsApp reminders go out before and after the due date, and the agency owner sees the aging report and collection rate. It ends with three "paid" notifications landing on three phones at once.
- First 2 s: three panes already moving, each with an orange "متأخرة" (overdue) pill on a real amount. Line: «فاتورة… ما انتدفعت.» ("An invoice… unpaid.")
- Beats: hook (3 panes) → tension (calls unanswered, days counter) → reveal (panes collapse into تحصيلي) → trading: Excel import → maintenance: reminder schedule + WhatsApp → agency: dashboard + aging → payoff (3 × "تم الدفع", "Paid") → claim → end card with "from AED 99" + "14 days free"
- Look: three worlds, each with its own colour and detail (Deira warehouse sand #f5e9d7 with cartons, Al Quoz workshop steel grey with a work order, Business Bay glass white with a laptop). The product world is light slate, and the end card is brand teal. IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- Sound: tense, rising music bed → drop on the reveal → light pulse in the proof scenes. SFX on every action (phone ring, click, notification).
- Length: 40–45 s.

## B. "خريطة التحصيل" (The collection map): Number lands × flight through one world (BOLD)
The camera flies over a stylised map of the UAE. Each red pin is an unpaid invoice: Deira, Al Quoz, Business Bay, Sharjah Industrial Area, Abu Dhabi Mussafah. Above the map is a running total of the money that's owed. At 9:00 AM Tahselly's daily run starts: teal lines go out from the system to every pin (email + WhatsApp). The pins turn green one after another, and the counter drops while "collected" climbs. Between stops the camera dives into one pin to show the real screen (import, schedule, dashboard), then flies back out.
- First 2 s: the map is already full of red pins, and the counter shows «166,040 درهم متأخرة» (166,040 AED overdue), the demo company's figure.
- Beats: hook (red map + counter) → tension (pins keep multiplying) → reveal (9:00, logo) → dive 1: import → dive 2: schedule + reminders → dive 3: dashboard → payoff (map turns green) → claim → end card
- Look: one map world, sand and teal, with real area names and pins as the only objects. The product shows inside each dive.
- Sound: a ticking clock in the hook, music that builds with each pin that turns green, a "ding" for each payment.
- Length: 35–45 s.

## C. "الساعة 9:00" (9:00 AM): A moment in someone's day × cut on the beat (TEASER)
The pure teaser version: almost nothing is shown at first. Kinetic type on the beat, each line a business name: «مكتب محاسبة… شركة صيانة… مطبعة… معهد تدريب… شركة تجارة…» (accounting office… maintenance company… print shop… training institute… trading company…). Then «كلهم عندهم نفس المشكلة.» ("They all have the same problem.") A clock moves to 9:00, and the moment it lands, quick hard cuts on the beat through the real screens: reminder sent → client opens it → payment recorded → dashboard. It ends with «ولا اتصال واحد.» ("Not a single call.") and the logo.
- First 2 s: a big word on a full-colour field: «مطبعة» (print shop), cutting to «شركة صيانة» (maintenance company) on the beat.
- Beats: hook (business names) → shared problem → clock to 9:00 → quick cuts through the product → claim → end card
- Look: flat colour fields (teal / sand / white), very large type, the product at full size in the cuts.
- Sound: a strong beat-driven track, so the edit relies entirely on the music.
- Length: 25–30 s (best for Instagram/TikTok, and could come out in a 9:16 version too).
