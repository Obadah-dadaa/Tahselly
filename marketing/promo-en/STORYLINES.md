# Tahseel promo — storylines

## A. "آخر الشهر" — A moment in someone's day × object-to-object chain (PICKED)
A finance manager at a Dubai marketing agency, the 27th of the month: an Excel sheet of invoices turning red row by row, a sticky note "call them — 4th time". The overdue total counts up to the real 166,040 AED. A teal field wipes in: تحصيل. Proof in the real UI as four verbs: upload Excel → set the schedule → reminders go out alone (email + WhatsApp) → record the payment, the overdue pill turns paid. Payoff: the owner dashboard, collection rate 91.2%. Roles flash (manager / owner / developer), end card with price.
- First 2 s: a spreadsheet already filling the frame, a row flashes red, the line "آخر الشهر."
- Beats: hook → tension (counter) → reveal → import → schedule → reminders → payment → payoff → roles → end card
- Look: white sheet world → brand teal field → light slate product world; IBM Plex Sans Arabic; teal accent rationed.
- Sound: no music source on this machine; bundled SFX on visible actions (click, pop, whoosh, riser into the payoff).

## B. "الرقم" — A number lands × one frame that rebuilds
One number on screen the whole film: 166,040 AED overdue. Every product action subtracts from it until it becomes the collected figure. Strong for accountants; weaker on showing the workflow.

## C. "قبل وبعد" — Life before and after × split screen to one
Left: phone calls, WhatsApp drafts, Excel filters. Right: Tahseel's daily 9:00 run. The split collapses into the owner dashboard. Clear, but risks the "comparison panels" tell.
