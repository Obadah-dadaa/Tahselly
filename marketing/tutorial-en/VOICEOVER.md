# Tutorial voiceover — English, detailed (temporary: en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural, to be replaced by HeyGen)

Story: the client company owns the account (Owner) and follows the results; its service partner does the daily work (Manager).

| id | scene | line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vo0 | intro | Welcome to Tahselly. This guide walks you through every part of the system, step by step, so your team and your service partner can work from the same account. |
| vo1 | roles | Here's how it works. Your service partner signs in as a Manager, and handles the daily work: clients, invoices, reminders and payments. Your company signs in as the Owner, and follows everything: the numbers, the reports, the settings and the subscription. Each person has their own login, so you always know who did what. |
| vo2 | login | Everyone signs in from the same page, with their own email and password. Owners land on the owner dashboard, and managers on the manager dashboard. |
| vo3 | team | First, the owner adds the team. Open Team. On the left, everyone who has access, their role, and when they last signed in. On the right, add a member: their name, email, and a temporary password. Then choose the role: Manager for your partner's staff, or Owner for people in your company who need full access. You can deactivate anyone, at any time. |
| vo4 | dashboard | The manager dashboard is the daily starting point. At the top: total outstanding, overdue, due in the next seven days, and collected this month. Below, the overdue invoices that need a follow-up, with the last reminder sent for each. |
| vo5 | clients | The Clients page lists every customer, with their open invoices, the amount outstanding, and whether reminders are on. Search by name, or click New client to add one. |
| vo6 | client-new | Enter the client's name, the contact person, the tax number, the email where reminders go, and their WhatsApp number. You can also choose the language of this client's reminders, Arabic or English, and switch automatic reminders on or off. |
| vo7 | invoice-new | To add an invoice, choose the client, enter the invoice number, the issue and due dates, and the amount, then save. Its reminders start automatically, on schedule. |
| vo8 | import | Have many invoices? Use Excel import. Drag your file here, just as it is, or download the template first. Tahselly reads the columns in Arabic or English, and creates the clients and invoices for you. |
| vo9 | sync | If you use an accounting system or an ERP, connect it once. Copy your private address, and have your system email its open invoices report to it every day. Tahselly adds new invoices, records payments, and closes what's been paid, on its own. You can also upload the report by hand to try it. |
| vo10 | invoices | The Invoices page shows every invoice. Filter by status: open, overdue, partly paid, paid or cancelled. Or search by number, client or date. Click any invoice to open it. |
| vo11 | invoice-show | On an invoice, the buttons at the top send an email or WhatsApp reminder right away, or a custom message. Below are the details: the due date, the amount paid, and the balance. And the reminder log shows every message sent, when, and by whom. |
| vo12 | invoice-pay | When the client pays, record it here: the amount, the date, the method, and a reference. A partial payment lowers the balance. A full payment closes the invoice and stops its reminders. You can also pause the reminders, or cancel the invoice. |
| vo13 | reminders | The reminders center explains how reminders work, and shows the automatic schedule. The tabs list what goes out in the coming days, what was scheduled by hand, and a log of everything sent. WhatsApp reminders are ready each day, one click each. |
| vo14 | reminder-new | To send a reminder yourself, click New reminder. Choose one or more invoices, then the channel, email or WhatsApp. Edit the message if you like, then send it now, or schedule it for later. |
| vo15 | aging | The receivables aging report shows how late your money is, from not yet due, to over ninety days. Export it to Excel in one click. |
| vo16 | settings | In Settings, the owner sets the reminder schedule: how many days before and after the due date, a final notice, the channels, and the default language. |
| vo17 | templates | The Message templates tab holds the wording of every reminder, by email and WhatsApp, in Arabic and English. Placeholders, like the client name and the balance, are filled in for each invoice. |
| vo18 | owner | As the owner, your dashboard gives you the full picture: receivables, collection rate, how many days it takes to get paid, and what was collected after reminders. Then invoiced against collected, the aging, and your top debtors. This is how you follow your partner's work. |
| vo19 | billing | The Subscription page shows your plan and when it ends. Renew or upgrade it in one click. |
| vo20 | switches | Finally, anyone can switch between Arabic and English, and between light and dark mode, from the top of the page. |
| vo21 | outro | That's it. Your partner does the follow-up, and you see the results. Welcome to Tahselly. |
